High Precision Wood Wool Cutter - ME Capstone

The HPWICT (High Precision Wood Wool Industrial Crafting Table) is a machine designed to shave wood into precisely dimensioned wool shavings for use in sustainable building materials. The project began with wood wool dimension predictions based on theoretical oblique cutting mechanics. Blade rake angle, inclination angle, and depth of cut were all varying parameters in this prediction. Ideal paramters were then implemented in the manufacturing of three high speed steel blades for cutting experiments.
